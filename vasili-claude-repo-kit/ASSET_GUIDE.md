# Asset guide

## Brand

`assets/brand/vasili-logo-white.svg` is the existing white vector logo from vasili.nyc. It is suitable for a dark background. Preserve its paths, proportions, and lettering. The supplied `references/logo-black-thick-original.jpg` is 173 × 72 pixels and entirely black; its filename does not mean the artwork is recoverable. Do not enlarge it or guess a replacement logo.

## Photography

`assets/photos/` contains original, unmodified downloaded photographs, deduplicated by file hash. See `data/assets.json` for dimensions, byte sizes, hashes, original URLs, and the extraction filenames consolidated into each file. All paths in the manifest are relative to the repository root.

- `DSC07321.png`: the vertical photograph of jewelry-covered hands over flame, also used as a crop in the unfinished-homepage screenshot. This is a large original; generate a web-sized derivative for page use.
- `One-Thirty_New_York0539.jpg`: the silver chain bracelet on white.
- Remaining files include studio and worn jewelry photographs. Inspect the actual images before placing them. Do not infer design ancestry or substitute one item for another merely because its filename is similar.

Product names, sales claims, historical text, and prices are not needed for this placeholder-only prototype. Source product groupings are provided as developer reference in `data/photo-groups.json`, not as approved display copy or origin-map connections.
